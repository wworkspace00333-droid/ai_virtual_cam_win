Camera test

Pick “AI Virtual Camera” (or “AI Virtual Camera (Windows Virtual Camera)”) and press Start. This is what a browser-based call (Google Meet, Teams on the web, Zoom in the browser) receives. Nothing leaves this computer.

  • The browser asks for camera permission the first time; allow it.
  • Only one app can control a Media Foundation camera at a time. If the camera is in use elsewhere, use the DirectShow camera (“AI Virtual Camera” without the suffix) or close the other app.
  • A dark “Waiting for AI Virtual Camera Studio…” card means the camera works but the app is not sending video: start processing in the app.
